### Color Scheme:
- Positive B∥: `#0066CC` (professional blue)
- Negative B∥: `#CC0000` (professional red)
- Reference: `#00CC00` (green)
- Grid: White/gray with low alpha for clarity

### Marker Sizes:
- ON points: 30 pt
- OFF points: 80 pt (larger to stand out)
- Edge colors: Black (0.5-1.0 pt) for clarity

This ensures all figures match journal publication standards while maintaining scientific clarity.
